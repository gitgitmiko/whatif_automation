Character design reference sheet on a plain light grey background, three views in one wide image: on the left a large front-facing close-up portrait of the face, in the middle the full body standing front view, on the right the same man sitting on his scooter. Same character in all three views. No text, no letters, no labels, no logos. He wears a plain dark grey t-shirt and dark jeans. His motorbike is a small automatic scooter in the style of a Honda BeAT, clean WHITE body with bright BLUE striping and blue accents, black seat.
